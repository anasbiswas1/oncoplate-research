# Paper reporting contract

The manuscript must follow actual results, not the optimism of the work plan. Proposed title: **OncoPlate: Evidence-Grounded Multimodal Recognition and Selective Guidance for Cancer-Relevant Dietary Choices**.

Required empirical story: valid documented reference standard; measured unsupported-assertion failure; a source-aware intervention; strong information-matched comparisons; external/prospective performance; constraints on app claims.

Required tables: collection flow with independent groups; actual field/claim support; reviewer disagreement and adjudication; rights/provenance; all registered seeds/backbones; shared-candidate M7–M3 contrast; rules-only and feature/capacity ablations; action budget and measured response burden; external outcomes; export/device parity; missing/failed runs. Report food and product domains separately and the registered 60:40 study mixture without claiming population prevalence.

The primary improvement criterion uses both the risk-difference interval and coverage-difference interval. Zero answers means undefined conditional risk, not zero error. Bootstrap independent acquisition groups, average the registered metric over seeds inside each resample, and report seed variability separately. More photographs, seeds, views or assay repeats do not increase the independent-subject count.

Open-ended wording, VLM-specific outputs and hierarchical coarsenings need independent ratings matching the actual output. Do not reuse a finer-grained label as proof that broader wording is correct. Do not derive reference support from the method's own eligibility rule.

Calibration diagnostics and public panel disagreement are not chemical or clinical truth. FoodNExTDB results remain the foundation; the newly documented benchmark is primary for the proposed flagship claim. A good benchmark can still be valuable when PCSI does not outperform a strong baseline. Do not change targets, select favourable seeds, claim first-ever status or suppress external degradation.

Figures and tables should be generated from saved protected predictions and independently adjudicated references. The reproduction notebook refuses to invent missing exhibits. App comprehension, real user behaviour, assay validation and cancer incidence are different evidence levels; report only those actually measured.
